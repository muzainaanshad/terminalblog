## Phase 4: HARO/Connectively (15 min/day)

### What is HARO?
- Journalists post queries like "looking for expert on AI tools"
- You respond with a quote → get featured in articles
- Sites like Forbes, TechCrunch, HubSpot use this

### How to use it:
1. Sign up at connectively.com (free)
2. Set alerts for: "AI", "coding", "developer tools", "software"
3. Check emails 2x daily (8 AM, 6 PM)
4. Respond to relevant queries within 1 hour
5. Include: your name, title, terminalblog.com

### Example query:
> "Looking for developers who use AI coding assistants daily. How has it changed your workflow?"

### Your response:
> "I've been using Claude Code and Codex daily for6 months. The biggest change is that I now spend more time on architecture and less on boilerplate. I track my results at terminalblog.com — the productivity gain is real but the learning curve is steep."

## Do's and Don'ts

### ✅ Do:
- Post genuinely helpful content
- Engage with other people's posts first
- Use consistent branding across platforms
- Track what content gets engagement
- Be patient (results take 4-8 weeks)

### ❌ Don't:
- Spam the same content everywhere
- Post only self-promotional content
- Use fake accounts or buy followers
- Expect overnight results
- Skip the engagement phase (comment on others first)
