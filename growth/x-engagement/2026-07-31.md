## TWEET 4 — Hot Take (AI Coding Agents)

> Hot take: The "agentic engineering" framing is wrong.
>
> It's not engineering with agents. It's product management with a compiler that writes code.
>
> The skill that matters isn't prompting. It's specifying — writing constraints tight enough that a model can't misinterpret them, then verifying with automated gates, not eyeballs.

> Most developers are not good at this. And that's the real hiring problem.
