### 4. HOT TAKE — AI Coding Agents

> The "AI coding agent" category is a trap. There are only three real categories:
> 
> 1. **Editor-native** (Cursor, Zed, Windsurf) — IDE does the reasoning
> 2. **Terminal agents** (Claude Code, Copilot CLI, OpenCode) — you drive, agent executes
> 3. **Unattended/scheduled** (Hermes, Devin) — agent runs while you sleep
> 
> Stop comparing across categories. Pick your *workflow*, then pick the tool.
> 
> Most "which agent is best" threads are category errors.
