## 3. HOT TAKE — AI coding agents

> The biggest lie in the agent debate isn't "they replace vs. don't replace devs."
>
> It's that more context = better agent performance.
>
> The data says the opposite: agents that trim context effectively (Aider's repo map, Hermes's selective context) cut costs 40-70% compared to naive full-context approaches.
>
> The skill isn't getting more context into the agent. It's keeping the right context in and the rest out.
>
> (No link — pure opinion/positioning.)

Alternative (if you want to go sharper):
> "Your coding agent re-reads the same 200 files every request. That's not intelligence — it's a billing strategy."
>
> Context management is the single biggest lever on your monthly agent bill and it's almost never in the feature comparison. The agents that get this right are the ones that actually ship at scale.
