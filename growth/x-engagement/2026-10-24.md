## 🔥 Hot Take (3/5)

**Hot take: "Your coding agent's default config is a supply chain attack waiting to happen."**

Every major agent (Claude Code, Goose, OpenCode, Hermes, Cursor) loads project-local config by default — hooks, MCP servers, env vars, custom tools. A cloned repo controls what runs on your machine **before you've read a single file**.

The fix isn't "be careful." It's: **project config must be opt-in, sandboxed, or signed.** Until then, `git clone` = `curl | sh` with extra steps.
