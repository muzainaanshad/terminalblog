### 4. Hot Take: AI Coding Agents

**Draft:**
Hot take: The "AI coding agent" category is splitting into two distinct products in 2026, and most devs haven't noticed.

Tier 1: **Interactive pair programmers** (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex) — you steer, they execute. Latency-sensitive, context-heavy.

Tier 2: **Autonomous engineering teams** (Kiro Crew, AmpCode Orbs, Devin) — you define outcomes, they coordinate multi-session work. Latency-tolerant, memory-heavy.

The mistake: using Tier 1 for Tier 2 jobs (overnight refactors, dependency migrations). The inverse mistake: paying Tier 2 prices for Tier 1 tasks (write a test, fix a typo).

Pick the tier, not the brand. The orchestration layer is where leverage lives now.
