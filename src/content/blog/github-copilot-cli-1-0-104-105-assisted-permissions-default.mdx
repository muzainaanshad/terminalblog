GitHub Copilot CLI v1.0.104 dropped **October 27**. v1.0.105 followed **50 minutes ago**. Two releases in 48 hours.

The headline isn't a new feature — it's a **graduation**. Assisted Permissions no longer require experimental mode. They're just… on.

---

## The One-Line Summary

**v1.0.105 (Oct 28) → v1.0.104 (Oct 27).** Assisted Permissions default-on. Fusion model auto-starts on Auto tier. Background tasks wait indefinitely in interactive/SDK Autopilot. Auto falls back gracefully. Fusion High/Max selectable from long-context sessions. Upgrade now.

---

## The Big One: Assisted Permissions Graduated

### What Changed

**v1.0.104:** `assistedApprovalInstructions` setting guides the judge. No `--experimental` flag needed. No config toggle. It works out of the box.

### Why It Matters

Since v1.0.101, Assisted Permissions existed but lived behind `--experimental`. You had to know the flag, enable it, tolerate rough edges. Now it's the default behavior.

```bash
# Before v1.0.104
copilot --experimental
# Then configure assisted permissions

# After v1.0.104
copilot
# Assisted permissions just work
```

The judge evaluates shell commands, MCP calls, and file operations — approving safe ones automatically, prompting only when risk crosses a threshold. Your `assistedApprovalInstructions` (added in v1.0.102) teach it your project's norms.

---

## Fusion Model Now Starts on Auto — Automatically

### What Changed

**v1.0.104:** The managed `"fusion"` model starts sessions on **Auto** tier and applies your `fusionTier` settings (High/Max) without manual selection.

### Why It Matters

Previously: you picked a model, or Auto picked something generic. Now Auto **means Fusion** — GitHub's routed model that picks the right backend (GPT, Claude, Gemini) per task.

```bash
# Your fusionTier in settings.json controls the ceiling
{
  "fusionTier": "Max"  # or "High"
}
```

Start a session. Fusion routes. You don't manage the model.

---

## Background Tasks Don't Time Out Anymore

### What Changed

**v1.0.104:** Interactive and SDK Autopilot sessions **keep waiting** for attached background tasks instead of stopping after `COPILOT_TASK_WAIT_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`.

The `-p --autopilot` flag still honors the timeout — scripted runs stay predictable. But interactive work? The agent finishes what it started.

### Why It Matters

You kick off a long refactor. Step away. Come back. The session is still there, still working. No more "where did my agent go?"

---

## Auto Falls Back Instead of Failing

### What Changed

**v1.0.104:** When your saved Auto preference becomes unavailable (model deprecated, region blocked, plan change), Auto **falls back to its default** instead of failing every turn.

### Why It Matters

Enterprise policy changes. Model rolls out. Region restrictions shift. Your sessions don't brick — they adapt.

---

## v1.0.105: Fusion High/Max Finally Works in Long Context

### What Changed

**v1.0.105 (50 mins ago):** Selecting **Copilot Fusion High or Max in `/model` now works from long-context sessions**.

### Why It Matters

Before: long-context sessions would ignore Fusion tier selection, defaulting to base. Now your tier choice respects context size. If you pay for Max, you get Max — even at 200k tokens.
