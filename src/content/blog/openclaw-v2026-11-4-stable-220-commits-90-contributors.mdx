**OpenClaw v2026.11.4 landed November 16** — and it's a proper stable release with numbers that demand attention: **220 commits, 125 pull requests, 90 contributors**. This isn't a patch. It's a milestone.

---

## The One-Line Summary

**OpenClaw v2026.11.4 is the largest stable drop yet — private subagent results stay private, child-task handoffs finally work reliably, tool failures are now visible instead of silent, and the beta channel is already testing Telegram widgets.**

---

## What Actually Changed (Stable v2026.11.4)

### Private Task Results — Subagent Completions Stay Out of Chat
Before: When a private subagent finished, its completion could leak into the main chat stream. Now: **private subagent completions are kept out of streamed chat replies entirely** (#188490).

If you run sensitive subagents (security audits, credential handling, internal analysis), their outputs won't accidentally surface in your conversation history.

### Reliable Child-Task Handoffs
The scenario: you cancel a child task, want to hand off to a replacement. Before: the cancelled child's completion receipt could **block the handoff**, leaving you stuck. Now: **parent can hand off to replacement work after cancelling a child, without the cancelled child's receipt blocking it** (#178893, #178565).

This makes multi-agent workflows actually composable — cancel, retry, replace, chain.

### Visible Tool Failures — No More Silent Ends
Before: An unanswered tool failure could silently end a tool use with no indication. Now: **unanswered tool failures are reported explicitly** instead of silently ending.

You'll know when something went wrong. No more wondering why the agent "just stopped."

## What's In Beta (v2026.11.5-beta.4)

The beta channel is already ahead with v2026.11.5-beta.4 (released same day, Nov 16):

### Telegram Widgets — Interactive Messages in Telegram
**Render interactive widgets as Telegram messages** (#196991). Your agent can now send buttons, forms, selectors, rich cards — not just text — directly in Telegram DMs.

### Large-Session Reliability (Beta Highlights)
- **Reduce memory pressure** during transcript indexing, trimming, compaction
- **Stream only selected hydration payloads** — honor configurable summary limits
- **Restart and delivery safety** — preserve queued-message permission ceilings, accepted ingress during restarts, drain final chat writes before handoff, retain delivery guarantees

### Paged History for Large Conversations (Beta 2+)
- **Large conversations remain browsable** with paged history in both directions
- **Visible history boundaries** and bounded archive reads
- No more "loading..." forever on 1000+ message threads

### macOS App Secret Preservation
- **Saved secrets and Gateway auth persist** when configuration saves fail or Tailscale exposure is toggled off
- No more re-entering credentials after a config hiccup

### Android Talk Fixes
- Ending Talk on Android no longer cancels background work incorrectly
