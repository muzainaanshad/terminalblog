OpenCode supports every major AI model provider. Claude, GPT-4, Gemini, local models through Ollama. Most coding agents lock you into one. OpenCode lets you switch freely.

This isn't just convenience — it's strategy. When Claude 4 drops, OpenCode users switch immediately. When GPT-4o gets cheaper, they switch again. No vendor negotiation, no platform migration, no feature loss.

The terminal-native design means OpenCode works where developers already work. No IDE to install, no browser extension to configure. It's a CLI tool that fits into existing workflows.

The 88% SWE-bench score proves the multi-provider approach doesn't sacrifice quality. OpenCode matches single-provider agents while offering flexibility they can't.

For developers who want choice — who don't want their workflow tied to one company's roadmap — OpenCode is the answer. The provider-neutral approach is the future of AI tools.
