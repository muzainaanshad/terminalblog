**Your coding agent can now build, test, and run iOS and macOS apps — even if you're on Windows or Linux. It spins up a Mac in the cloud, does the work, streams the screen to your thread, and cleans up after itself.**

---

## What macOS Satellites Actually Are

Before this, AmpCode tried "Morbs" — orbs that ran the entire thread on a Mac. It failed: Docker and backends took minutes to start, and keeping Mac disks around got expensive.

**The fix:** Split the thread across two machines.

- **Your orb (Linux)** — runs the thread, dev server, toolchain, database, everything that works on Linux. Starts fast. Stays running.
- **macOS Satellite (Mac)** — only appears when the work needs macOS: Xcode builds, simulator runs, device tests. Boots in ~30 seconds. Syncs code from the orb. Returns screenshots, logs, video. Deletes itself after 20 minutes of inactivity.

They're called *Satellites* because they orbit your orb.

## What You Can Do With a Satellite

### Build and Test iOS Apps

Point Amp at your iOS project. It:
1. Starts a Satellite (`mac.medium` — 6 CPU, 14 GB, $2/hr)
2. Syncs your code
3. Runs `xcodebuild` or `fastlane`
4. Launches the simulator (iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone Duo, iPad, whatever)
5. Streams the screen to your thread — you watch the app run
6. You can *take control* of the Mac and tap the simulator yourself

### Build and Test macOS Apps

Same flow. Native Mac apps. Amp drove the iPod app for Mac, changed the model to "U2 iPod," played The Avalanches — all recorded and reported back.

### Fix Layout Bugs Across Form Factors

The demo shows Amp checking an app on iPhone 17 Pro, then iPhone Duo (foldable), fixing the layout for both, unfolding the Duo, tapping a transaction — all automated, all visible in the thread.

## The UX: A "Mac" Tab in Your Thread

When a Satellite is active, your thread gets a **Mac tab**. It streams the Mac's screen live.

- **Watch** — See what the agent is doing in real time
- **Take control** — Click into the stream, use the Mac yourself, hand back to the agent
- **Artifacts** — Screenshots, build logs, test results, video recordings all land in the thread
