## Pros & Cons

### OpenCode
**Pros:**
- Multi-provider support
- Free and open source
- Skill-driven execution

**Cons:**
- Archived / no active development
- No vision support

### Oh My Pi
**Pros:**
- Largest model catalog
- Vision and browser automation
- Daily releases (v16+)

**Cons:**
- No built-in cron
- Less polished TUI
