Kraken has relaunched its mobile application with a headline feature that sets it apart from every other crypto exchange app: **native agentic trading bots**. Users can now deploy, configure, and monitor autonomous trading strategies directly from the Kraken mobile interface without writing a single line of code.

The trading agents operate on configurable parameters — entry and exit signals, position sizing, stop-loss logic, and portfolio rebalancing triggers — all managed through a visual builder. Once activated, the agents execute trades autonomously around the clock, reacting to market conditions faster than any human trader could.

This is part of a broader push across fintech to embed AI agents directly into consumer financial products. Kraken's move is significant because it bypasses the traditional intermediary layer — users previously needed third-party bot platforms or API-connected scripts to run automated strategies. Now the agent is a first-class feature of the exchange itself.

The timing aligns with the broader industry shift toward **agent-as-feature** rather than agent-as-separate-product. Whether in trading, coding, or communications, the pattern is the same: platforms that embed agents directly win on convenience and retention.
