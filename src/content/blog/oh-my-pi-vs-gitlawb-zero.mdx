## Open Source vs Commercial

The open source vs commercial debate matters more for coding agents than most tools because your agent has access to everything.

**Oh My Pi** is built for model exploration and experimentation. It's open source with Free (BYO keys) pricing. 

**Gitlawb Zero** is built for developers who want full ownership. It's open source with Free (BYO keys) pricing.

## Pros & Cons

### Oh My Pi
**Pros:**
- Largest model catalog
- Vision and browser automation
- Daily releases (v16+)

**Cons:**
- No built-in cron
- Less polished TUI

### Gitlawb Zero
**Pros:**
- Full ownership, no vendor lock-in
- Customizable keyboard shortcuts
- Android Termux support

**Cons:**
- No vision
- No subagents
