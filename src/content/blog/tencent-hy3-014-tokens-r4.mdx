Tencent has launched **Hy3**, a coding-focused AI model priced at **$0.14 per million input tokens** — a number that should make every team paying premium rates for code generation take a second look. In a market where the big-name models charge multiples of that for similar work, Hy3 is positioning itself as the budget engine for developers who burn through tokens by the billion.

The model targets the bread-and-butter of software work: writing functions, refactoring modules, explaining unfamiliar code, and turning natural-language specs into working pull requests. Tencent is betting that for most coding tasks, raw flagship performance is overkill, and a lean model at a fraction of the cost wins on total bill rather than leaderboard bragging rights.

The pricing is notable at scale. A team running thousands of agentic edits a day can watch its monthly API spend collapse when the per-token rate drops this low — which lowers the barrier to letting agents handle more of the backlog instead of rationing their calls.

The catch is quality on hard problems. Cheap tokens are worthless if the model fumbles architecture or subtle bugs. But if Hy3 holds up on routine engineering, it sets a new price floor the rest of the field will feel. For cost-conscious shops, the calculus just shifted.
