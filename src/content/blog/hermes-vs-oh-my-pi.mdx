## Pros & Cons

### Hermes Agent
**Pros:**
- Cron and scheduling built-in
- Multi-provider routing
- Subagent delegation

**Cons:**
- Requires configuration
- Less polished than commercial agents

### Oh My Pi
**Pros:**
- Largest model catalog
- Vision and browser automation
- Daily releases (v16+)

**Cons:**
- No built-in cron
- Less polished TUI
