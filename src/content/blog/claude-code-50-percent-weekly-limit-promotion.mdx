**Q1: Do I need to do anything to get the 50% weekly boost?**
No. The increase is applied automatically to eligible plans (Pro, Max, Team, and legacy seat-based Enterprise). Run `/usage` in the CLI to confirm the higher weekly limit is showing on your account.

**Q2: Does this promotion raise my 5-hour usage limit too?**
No. The promotion increases **weekly** usage limits only. The 5-hour rolling limit is unchanged, so short-window throttling is not affected.

**Q3: What happens to my limit after July 19, 2026?**
Weekly usage limits in Claude Code return to their standard levels. There is no change to your plan or billing — the boost simply expires at 11:59 PM PT on July 19, 2026.
