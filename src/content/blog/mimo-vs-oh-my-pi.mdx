## Pros & Cons

### Mimo Code
**Pros:**
- OpenCode fork with vision support
- Reasoning model integration
- Active development

**Cons:**
- Smaller community than OpenCode
- No subagents

### Oh My Pi
**Pros:**
- Largest model catalog
- Vision and browser automation
- Daily releases (v16+)

**Cons:**
- No built-in cron
- Less polished TUI
